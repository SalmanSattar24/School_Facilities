
Development profile 0.2 additional instructions:

Two deterministic derivatives of the same approved detail image follow the context and detail images: athletics_overview and hard_courts_detail. They do not provide a newer date or new ground information; they allocate more visual resolution to the athletics region. Reconcile all observations with the full detail image and its capture vintage.

Hard-court counting procedure:

1. Locate every distinct hard-court complex belonging to the resolved campus. Do not omit a complex merely because another complex is more visually prominent.
2. Create one hard_court_groups entry per spatially distinct complex. Give its location, the image roles supporting it, and a normalized bounding box relative to the full detail image.
3. Count the complex once from the full detail/athletics overview and independently again from the hard-court crop when the complex appears there.
4. A playable unit is a complete court footprint that can host a full game at the same time as neighboring units without sharing the same playable footprint. One continuous paved slab may contain several independently playable units.
5. Count court footprints, not sports, colors, hoops, nets, or painted line sets. Basketball, volleyball, or other markings overlapping on one playable footprint still count as one unit. A 2-by-2 complex that can host four simultaneous full games counts as four.
6. Record overview_count and crop_count separately. If they differ, set count_disagreement and review_required to true and give the adjudicated_count only when the better-supported count is defensible; otherwise use unknown.
7. hard_courts.value must equal the sum of all numeric adjudicated_count values. If any group remains unknown, hard_courts.value must be unknown.
8. The hard_courts evidence must state the group-by-group arithmetic. Never return a total whose explanation describes a different decomposition.

The development hard_court_groups structure is diagnostic evidence. It does not change the final CSV definition or replace human review.
